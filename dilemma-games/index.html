The dictator's dilemma: two games

POLI6015, Week 1. One projector screen run by the seminar leader; students play on their phones. Draft for Jamie: not yet used with students.

Every student is a numbered province with a secret harvest. They report it to the Chairman, first with nothing at stake, then under a quota that punishes the lowest. The state takes grain on the strength of the reports. Two fixes follow: rival inspectors, and a premium for honesty that the Chairman may not pay. About 30 minutes, one phone per student.

Wintrobe (1998), ch. 1: fear makes reports false (p. 22); the enforcement problem (pp. 25–26); competing agencies (pp. 34–35).

One team rules; four teams are provinces with a hidden grievance; the Police Chief has a private phone. Each year the Palace spends 8 tokens on gifts, police, petition boxes, the Chief or a Swiss account, and the provinces praise, stay silent, petition or protest. Do you open the box? About 35 minutes over four years, one phone per team plus one for the Chief.

Wintrobe (1998), ch. 1: the paradox and the subject's dilemma (p. 22); repression and loyalty (p. 33); the security forces as the main threat (p. 34); rents (p. 36); power traded for security (p. 38).
